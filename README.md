# TheGoodIntro

An invite-only Australian network that connects vetted software vendors with
senior executives for a single, qualified meeting. Every meeting funds a gift to
a registered charity the executive chooses. Executives pay nothing.

Live site: https://thegoodintro.vercel.app

## The idea

Senior leaders' calendars are full of cold pitches that go nowhere. TheGoodIntro
flips that. A vendor cannot request a meeting cold. They must state the specific
initiative or business challenge that makes the conversation relevant, so the
executive sees the reason before they accept. The few conversations worth having
also send a real gift to a cause the executive cares about.

## How it works

1. **The executive sets relevance.** They join free and tell us the priorities
   they will take conversations about. Nothing outside that reaches them.
2. **The vendor qualifies the ask.** A vendor states the specific initiative or
   problem. That context is shown to the executive, who decides freely, with no
   obligation.
3. **The conversation funds a cause.** One focused conversation happens, and a
   real gift goes to the executive's chosen registered Australian charity.

## How the money works

The vendor pays a flat $1,500 per held meeting. No-shows are not charged. The
executive pays nothing, ever.

The full fee is TheGoodIntro's revenue, and from it we donate a fixed gift to the
executive's chosen registered charity. The gift rises with how many meetings the
vendor takes across the year:

| Meetings per year | Gift to chosen charity |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | $900 |
| 6–10 | $1,000 |
| 11–15 | $1,100 |
| 16 or more | $1,200 |

We publish the split. The full model, including the tax treatment, lives in
[CHARITY_FLOW.md](CHARITY_FLOW.md).

## What makes it different

- **Qualified by requirement.** Access is earned, not bought. A vendor states a
  specific reason a conversation is worth the executive's time, or it never
  reaches them.
- **A deliberately big gift.** The gift is set high on purpose, so the giving
  means something rather than being a token gesture.
- **No fine print.** The full money flow is published. No vague claims, nothing
  buried in terms.

## Must-keep feature for v1: public impact dashboard

A shareable page per executive showing cumulative donations directed through the
platform, for example "Sarah has directed $14,000 to Beyond Blue via TheGoodIntro
meetings". Designed to be shareable on LinkedIn.

This is a primary growth and retention lever, not a nice to have. It drives
distribution and gives executives social proof plus an ongoing reason to keep
taking meetings. It is on the v1 spec and is not deferred to a later release.
