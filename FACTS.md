# TheGoodIntro — House Facts (canonical; read before writing any copy or numbers)

This is the single source of truth for the facts that keep getting written wrong
from stale memory or old docs. **If anything anywhere (a brief, an older doc, a
memory, existing repo text) conflicts with this file on these facts, this file
wins.** Customer-facing copy, UI strings, and emails must match this exactly.

Why this exists: the repo carries the project's old lowercase name and older
pricing in ~88 files. When copy is generated by mirroring the repo, it inherits
those mistakes. Check here first, not the surrounding code.

## Brand name

- The brand is always **TheGoodIntro**: capital **T**, capital **G**, capital
  **I**, one word. Use this in all customer-facing copy, UI text, emails, page
  titles, and any new prose. (Locked 2026-05-25.)
- The lowercase **theGoodintro** appears throughout the repo in file paths, the
  repo slug, package scopes (`@thegoodintro/*`), domains, and older docs. That is
  **legacy and is not authoritative for copy.** Do not bulk-rename code, paths, or
  domains (that breaks things and is not wanted), but **never emit lowercase
  "theGoodintro" in generated copy or UI.**
- Wordmark: the "Good" is the emerald highlight. In rich brand contexts the
  capital "Good" carries the emerald accent.
- Domain: **thegoodintro.com** (singular). The Google workspace is
  thegoodintros.com (plural). This mismatch is known and intentional; do not flag it.

## Pricing and charity amounts (NEVER quote from memory)

- The **only** sources of truth are [`apps/web/app/pricing/page.tsx`](apps/web/app/pricing/page.tsx)
  and [`CALCULATIONS.md`](CALCULATIONS.md). Read one of them before stating any price
  or charity figure. Do not take pricing from any other doc, brief, or memory.
- In customer copy, prefer "a real gift" or "the full gift" over a hard number
  unless a number is essential. If you must quote a figure, read it off the source
  first.

Current canonical model (still verify against the source before shipping copy):

- Fee: **$1,500 AUD per meeting, ex GST.** GST is $150 (10%). Total invoiced per
  meeting is $1,650. The fee is a fixed constant; no comped or free meetings.
- The charity share rises with the vendor's cumulative meetings in their annual
  cycle:

  | band | meetings (cumulative) | to charity | TheGoodIntro keeps |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1 to 5 | $900 | $600 |
  | 2 | 6 to 10 | $1,000 | $500 |
  | 3 | 11 to 15 | $1,100 | $400 |
  | 4 | 16 and above | $1,200 | $300 |

- Headline phrasing: "between $900 and $1,200 of each $1,500 reaches the charity
  the executive chooses," rising to about 70% of fees by 20 cumulative meetings.
  The rate applies within each band, never retroactively. Credits roll over; the
  tier resets each calendar year at the $900 band.
- Charity-flow model: the **donation model** (TheGoodIntro earns the $1,500,
  donates the band amount, claims the deduction, charges GST on the full fee; the
  vendor gets a business-expense receipt, not a gift receipt). See
  [`CHARITY_FLOW.md`](CHARITY_FLOW.md). This is final.
