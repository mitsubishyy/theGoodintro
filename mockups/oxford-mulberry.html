Why this exists

A senior leader's calendar is full of pitches that go nowhere.

What if the few meetings actually worth taking also did real good. That is the whole idea. Fewer, sharper conversations, each one turned into a meaningful donation.

How it works

Three steps, for executives.

1

You set what is relevant

Tell us the priorities and challenges you actually want to talk about. Nothing else gets through.

2

You get qualified requests

A vendor must state the specific initiative or problem before they can ask. You see the reason first.

3

One focused meeting

You take a single, well prepared conversation, and a substantial donation goes to your chosen charity.

Where the money goes

Full transparency, by design.

To your chosen charity, per meeting $1,000
Platform admin fee charged separately and named, never taken from the donation
Focus at launch Australia first

The donation figure stays clean and whole. Running costs are recovered through a clearly labelled admin fee, not skimmed from what reaches the charity.

What makes this different

Built around relevance and honesty.

Qualified by requirement

No cold asks. A vendor must show why the meeting is worth your time before it reaches you.

A deliberately larger gift

The donation is set high on purpose, so the impact is real, not a token.

Nothing hidden

The full money flow is on the page. No vague claims, no fine print games.

Questions
