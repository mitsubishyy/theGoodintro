# Claude Design prompt — TheGoodIntro charity partnership deck

Paste everything below the line into Claude Design (claude.ai/design).
It is a reusable template: build it once, then duplicate and swap the
`[CHARITY NAME]` placeholders + photos for each of the 15 charities.

---

Build me a clean, presentation-grade slide deck (a self-contained HTML keynote,
one `<section>` per slide at 1920×1080, advanced with the left/right arrow keys,
with a slide counter and an optional speaker-notes overlay toggled by pressing N,
and a print stylesheet so it exports cleanly to PDF — one slide per landscape
page). It must look like a calm, premium, founder-to-founder document, NOT a
busy corporate pitch. One idea per slide. Lots of breathing room.

This deck is sent BY TheGoodIntro TO a charity, to invite them to be one of the
causes featured on our website and to ask permission to use their name, logo and
public information. The audience is a charity partnerships / brand person. Tone:
warm, respectful, transparent, no hype. We are a small Australian company being
straight with them.

## Brand and visual system (match these exactly)

Fonts (Google Fonts): **Inter** (400/500/600/700) for headlines and body,
**Fraunces** italic (500/600) for ONE emphasis word per headline and for the
wordmark, **JetBrains Mono** (500) for small uppercase eyebrow labels only.

Colours (use only these — emerald is the single accent, no other colours, no
gradients, no gold, no blue, no purple):
- Deep frame / letterbox: `#14110c`
- Warm paper backgrounds, alternate down the deck between: `#F6F3EA`, `#EFEADD`, `#F1ECDF`, `#EDE7D8`
- Ink (headlines/body): `#1E1B17`
- Muted ink (sub-copy): `#5A554C`
- Label grey (eyebrows): `#8A8276`
- Emerald accent (the only colour): `#157852`

Recurring elements on most slides:
- A small **pulsing emerald dot** (9px, with a soft expanding-ring CSS pulse
  animation) next to a **JetBrains Mono uppercase eyebrow label**, ~18px,
  letter-spacing 0.18em, colour `#8A8276`.
- Headlines in Inter 600, large (50–100px depending on length), tight
  letter-spacing (-0.02 to -0.025em), with exactly **one word** set in
  Fraunces italic 500 in emerald `#157852` for emphasis.
- The wordmark rendered as `TheGoodIntro` where "The" and "Intro" are ink
  `#1E1B17` and "Good" is emerald `#157852`, set in Fraunces 600. Always write
  the brand as **TheGoodIntro** (capital T, G, I — never lowercase).

Hard copy rules: **no em dashes or en dashes** anywhere in the text (use a full
stop or restructure; en dash only inside a numeric range like 900–1,200). Never
the word "marketplace". No emojis. No timers/urgency. Plain, confident, warm.

## Slides (build these in order)

**Slide 1 — Cover.**
Eyebrow: "CHARITY PARTNERSHIP". Big wordmark. Headline: "The introduction that
*gives* back." (italic emerald word: "gives"). Sub-line: "Prepared for [CHARITY
NAME]." Footer rule: "Issy Hardwick, Founder, TheGoodIntro · [MONTH] 2026".

**Slide 2 — What we are, in one sentence.**
Eyebrow: "WHAT WE DO". One short, plain-English headline a busy person gets in
five seconds: "TheGoodIntro is an invite-only network where senior executives
take a meeting, and every meeting sends a real *gift* to a charity they choose."
(italic word: "gift"). Below it, three tiny mono-labelled supporting lines, no
paragraphs: "INVITE-ONLY · senior Australian leaders" / "EVERY MEETING · funds a
real charitable gift" / "THE EXECUTIVE CHOOSES · their own cause". Keep it
airy.

**Slide 3 — How a meeting becomes a gift (simple 3-step).**
Eyebrow: "THE MODEL". Headline: "Three steps, no fine print." Three numbered
steps across the slide, each one line: 1. "A vetted company books a meeting with
a senior leader." 2. "The leader nominates a DGR-endorsed Australian charity."
3. "We pay that charity a real gift, confirmed in writing within 14 days." Keep
each step to a single sentence with a big emerald number.

**Slide 4 — How we support you, and the money.**
Eyebrow: "WHAT THIS MEANS FOR [CHARITY NAME]". Headline: "Every booked meeting
can become a gift to *you*." (italic word: "you"). Then ONE clear money block,
large and unmissable:
- Big number: "$900 to $1,200" with caption "to the chosen charity, per meeting".
- Supporting line: "Out of a $1,500 meeting fee, $900 to $1,200 goes straight to
  the executive's chosen charity. That is up to 80% of the fee." 
  [ISSY: confirm this % — source of truth is $900–$1,200 of $1,500, i.e. 60–80%.
  If you want to quote a single conservative headline number, edit it here.]
- A small reassurance line: "The gift is paid in full even if the meeting goes
  badly. No conditions on the charity's side, ever."
Do NOT clutter this slide with the full pricing tiers. One number, one promise.

**Slide 5 — How we'd feature you (overview + photo).**
Eyebrow: "ON OUR WEBSITE". Headline: "We'd give [CHARITY NAME] a real home on
our site." Left column: 3 short bullet lines of what the feature includes (a
dedicated card in our giving gallery; your mission in your own words; a photo
that shows your work). Right column: a large placeholder image area labelled
"[PHOTO 1 — your work in the field]" with a subtle B&W-to-colour treatment.
Leave the image as a clearly-marked placeholder box.

**Slide 6 — Your individual page (mockup, photos).**
Eyebrow: "YOUR PAGE". Headline: "A page built *for* you." (italic: "for"). Show
a stylised browser/card mockup of the individual charity page we build (header
with your logo, a short purpose statement, two or three programme highlights, a
quote, and a photo). Use two placeholder image boxes: "[LOGO — supplied by you,
high-res]" and "[PHOTO 2 — a moment that shows your impact]". A caption note:
"Logos are shown crisp and high-resolution, never stretched or blurred."

**Slide 7 — Part of a considered group (roster + photo).**
Eyebrow: "THE COMPANY YOU'D KEEP". Headline: "Chosen alongside Australia's most
*trusted* causes." (italic: "trusted"). A tidy grid of 15 small placeholder
logo tiles labelled "[CHARITY LOGO]" (this is the cohort of 15 you've chosen).
One photo placeholder: "[PHOTO 3 — optional, your community]". Sub-line: "Every
charity we feature is DGR-endorsed and chosen with care. These are illustrative
of the causes executives can give to; the executive always chooses."

**Slide 8 — The honest part: we are a business.**
Eyebrow: "FULL TRANSPARENCY". Headline: "We are a for-profit company, and we are
proud of how we *share* it." (italic: "share"). Plain copy: "TheGoodIntro is not
a charity and not a fundraiser. We are a commercial business that earns a fee for
each meeting. We choose to give the majority of that fee away. Up to $1,200 of
every $1,500, up to 80%, goes to the charity the executive chose."
[ISSY: same % to confirm as slide 4 — keep them identical.] Add a quiet closing
line: "We tell you this up front because trust is the whole product."

**Slide 9 — What we'd need from you.**
Eyebrow: "THE ASK". Headline: "What we'd need from *you*." (italic: "you").
Three clean items, each a single line with a small emerald check or number:
1. "Permission to use your name and logo on our website."
2. "A high-resolution logo, and a short description of your work (or permission
   to use what is already public on your site)."
3. "One or two photographs we can feature, or the okay to use images from your
   public materials." 
Close with: "That is all. No cost, no exclusivity, no commitment on your side."

**Slide 10 — Closing / what happens next.**
Eyebrow: "NEXT STEP". Headline: "Let's give your cause a *bigger* table."
(italic: "bigger"). One sentence: "If this sounds right, reply and we'll send a
one-page agreement and build your page within the week." Footer: wordmark +
"Issy Hardwick, Founder · hello@thegoodintro.com".

## Layout discipline
Alternate the warm paper background tone on every consecutive slide so no two
neighbours share the same shade. Never put two text-heavy slides back to back
without a photo or number slide between them. Keep every slide to one headline +
at most three supporting lines. White space is the premium signal.
