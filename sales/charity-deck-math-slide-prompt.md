Add one slide to my deck, matching the existing style exactly.

Design system: warm paper background `#F1ECDF` (or alternate with `#EFEADD` so it
doesn't match its neighbour). Headlines Inter 600, ink `#1E1B17`, with one word
in Fraunces italic 500 emerald `#157852`. Eyebrow label in JetBrains Mono,
uppercase, letter-spacing 0.18em, colour `#8A8276`, preceded by a small pulsing
emerald dot. Body/sub-copy in muted ink `#5A554C`. Emerald `#157852` is the only
accent. Brand written as TheGoodIntro (capital T, G, I; "Good" in emerald).
No em or en dashes (en dash only inside a numeric range). 1920x1080.

Eyebrow: "THE MATH". Headline: "What a year could put behind *your* cause."
(italic emerald word: "your").

Then a clean three-part money layout, generous spacing, no clutter:

**1. The starting pledge (left or top, as a bold callout):**
Big number "$10,000". Caption under it: "Our founding pledge to [CHARITY NAME],
to start your tally the day you join." Keep this visually distinct, like a
sealed commitment.

**2. The rate (middle):**
A simple line of fact: "Then every meeting that names you sends $900 to $1,200
straight to [CHARITY NAME]. About $1,000 a meeting." Small note beneath in mono:
"THE GIFT RISES AS A VENDOR BOOKS MORE MEETINGS."

**3. The year-one illustration (right or bottom):**
A small, clearly-labelled illustrative build-up (mark it "Illustrative" in small
mono so it never reads as a promise). Show three tidy rows:
  - "$10,000  founding pledge"
  - "+ $20,000  from a year of meetings that choose you" 
       (sub-note: "around 20 meetings at roughly $1,000 each")
  - A thin emerald rule, then the total: "$30,000+  behind [CHARITY NAME] in
    year one, from TheGoodIntro alone."
Make the final "$30,000+" the largest emerald-accented number on the slide.

Close with one quiet line at the bottom: "Every dollar is a real gift to a real
charity, paid and confirmed in writing. The more our network grows, the more
this number does."

Keep it calm and uncluttered: one pledge, one rate, one illustrated total. White
space is the premium signal. Do not add charts or pricing tables.
