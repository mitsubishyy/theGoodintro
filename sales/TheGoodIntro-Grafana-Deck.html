The Problem

Senior leaders don't answer cold outreach.

Your team spends most of its cycles chasing meetings that never land, or land at the wrong level. The conversations that actually convert are warm, relevant introductions. But warm introductions don't scale, and a great SDR can only know so many people.

What This Is

An invite-only network of introductions that fund real Good.

Senior executives agree to take qualified meetings. Every meeting they take sends a real gift to a charity they choose. For a vendor, it is a way to reach senior leaders by being relevant, and fund something real while you do it.

The considered alternative to cold outreach.
